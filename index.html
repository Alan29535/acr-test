Video Quality Rating

This is a short video quality study at Chulalongkorn University. You will watch 18 short videos (10 seconds each, about 6 minutes in total) and rate the picture quality of each one.

No personal information is collected. Your answers are stored under a random ID. Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time by closing this page; nothing is submitted until the end.

Please use a laptop or desktop computer in a quiet room, set the screen to full brightness, and sit at a normal viewing distance. Please take part only once.

You seem to be using a phone or tablet. Small screens hide differences between videos, so please open this link on a computer if you can.

About 23 MB of video is loaded into your browser for smooth playback; nothing is saved to your device.

Instructions

Each video plays once, in full screen. After it ends, rate the overall picture quality:

5 Excellent · 4 Good · 3 Fair · 2 Poor · 1 Bad

There are no right or wrong answers; give your first impression. The first 3 videos are practice to show you the range of quality.

Practice finished
